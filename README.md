## 🔬 Research & Innovation

</div>

### 🏆 Privicore AI

**Differentially Private Federated Active Learning Auction System**

A privacy-preserving learning framework that trains shared models across siloed datasets while using an auction mechanism to identify valuable data for labeling — without raw data leaving its owner.

`Indian Provisional Patent Application`

---

### 📄 HealthPredict AI

**AI-Driven Risk Prediction System for Chronic Diseases Using Supervised Learning on Electronic Health Records**

A supervised-learning framework for chronic-disease risk prediction from Electronic Health Records, designed around explainable and clinically relevant outputs.

`Published Research — IJRTI`
